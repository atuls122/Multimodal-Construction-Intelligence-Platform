# Conclusion

## What was built
A multimodal Construction Intelligence Platform that combines computer vision (Gemini), a transparent
calculation engine and a rule-based advisory engine in one Streamlit app. It audits site safety and
cracks from photos, estimates budget, timeline and asset value, and turns weather and analysis results
into prioritised actions, with a chat assistant on top.

## What was achieved
- Image-based safety and crack analysis returning structured results
- Editable, explainable budget and timeline model
- AI explanations and cost-saving suggestions
- Advisory engine (STOP / HIGH / MEDIUM / INFO) and context-aware chat assistant
- Automated tests, manual test plan and a free cloud deployment route

## Limitations
- Cost rates and quantity norms are rule-of-thumb figures, not live market prices
- AI image analysis from a single photo cannot replace an on-site engineer's inspection
- Depends on internet access and the Gemini free-tier limits
- No user accounts or saved history

## Future scope
- Live material price feeds and city-wise rate tables
- Photo-over-time tracking of crack growth and construction progress
- PDF report export, multi-language support (Hindi), offline mode
- Drawing / floor-plan reading for automatic quantity take-off
- Database for projects and team collaboration
